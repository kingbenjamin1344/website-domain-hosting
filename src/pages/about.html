Who We Are

GH MAX CORPORATION is a retail business based in Davao City, providing products and practical solutions for individuals, schools, offices, and businesses.

With more than 40 years of service, we continue to support our customers through a range of school supplies, office supplies, furniture, equipment, and other essential products.

Our History

For more than 40 years, GH has been serving the school, office, and business needs of its customers. Through years of service and customer relationships, the company continues to provide essential products and practical solutions for everyday activities.
