Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how GH MAX CORPORATION handles information in connection with this website and when you contact us.

1. Overview

GH MAX CORPORATION (“GH MAX,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy applies to your use of this website and explains what information may be handled when you browse the website or contact us through the contact details published here.

This website is intended to provide general information about GH MAX, our company, and our products and services. The website's Contact page provides direct contact options, including email, telephone, and Facebook. It does not currently provide a website contact form.

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Philippine laws, including the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its implementing rules and regulations, and applicable guidance issued by the National Privacy Commission.

2. Information we may receive

We do not ask visitors to submit personal information through a contact form on this website. However, we may receive information that you choose to provide when you contact us directly, such as:

  • your name and contact details, such as your email address or telephone number;
  • the contents of your email, call, or Facebook message;
  • business or inquiry details that you choose to share with us; and
  • other information you voluntarily provide when communicating with GH MAX.

When you visit the website, the website host or security services may also process limited technical information needed to deliver, maintain, and protect the website. Depending on the service and configuration, this may include IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, timestamps, and security or diagnostic logs. We do not use this policy to claim that every such data point is collected in every visit.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your inquiry and you have an appropriate reason to share it.

3. How we use information

Information you provide may be used, as appropriate, to:

  • respond to your questions, requests, or business inquiries;
  • communicate with you about the matter you raised;
  • evaluate and follow up on a product, service, or business inquiry;
  • maintain the security and proper operation of the website;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests; and
  • establish, exercise, or defend legal claims where necessary.

We do not state that we use information submitted through this website for automated profiling, targeted advertising, or marketing subscriptions. If our practices change, we will update this policy and provide any notice or choice required by applicable law.

4. Disclosure and service providers

We do not sell personal information collected through this website. Information may be disclosed only when reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including:

  • to authorized GH MAX personnel who need the information to handle your inquiry;
  • to service providers that support website hosting, security, communications, or business operations, subject to appropriate safeguards;
  • to government authorities, regulators, courts, or other parties when disclosure is required or permitted by law; or
  • where necessary to protect the rights, safety, property, or legitimate interests of GH MAX, visitors, or others.

Where a service provider processes personal information on our behalf, that processing should be limited to the services it provides and governed by appropriate contractual and security arrangements.

5. Cookies and technical data

Cookies and similar technologies may be used by website infrastructure or security services to support essential functions, reliability, and protection against abusive traffic. The use of such technologies can depend on the services enabled and their configuration.

This policy does not represent that analytics, advertising, or other optional tracking tools are active. If GH MAX adds analytics, advertising technologies, embedded media, or other tools that collect information beyond essential website operations, this policy should be reviewed and updated before those tools are enabled.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect the operation of some website features or security checks.

6. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was obtained, including responding to an inquiry, maintaining appropriate business records, meeting legal or regulatory requirements, and resolving disputes. The appropriate retention period depends on the type of information and the circumstances of the interaction.

We take reasonable organizational, physical, and technical measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No website, email transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your privacy rights

Subject to the conditions and limitations under applicable law, individuals may have rights concerning their personal information, including the right to be informed, access, correct, object to certain processing, request erasure or blocking where legally appropriate, and seek remedies for violations of their privacy rights. The availability of a particular right depends on the circumstances and applicable legal requirements.

To make a privacy-related request, contact GH MAX using the details below. We may need to verify your identity and obtain additional information to understand and process your request. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

You may also lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission if you believe your personal information has been mishandled. More information is available at privacy.gov.ph.

8. Third-party websites and platforms

This website may link to third-party websites or platforms, including Facebook and map services. When you follow an external link, the third party's own terms and privacy policy apply to your activity on that service. GH MAX does not control the privacy, security, or content practices of third-party services.

If you contact GH MAX through Facebook, your interaction is also subject to the platform's terms, settings, and privacy practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, business operations, service providers, or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Please review this page periodically.
